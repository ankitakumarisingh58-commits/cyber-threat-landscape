Introduction

Cybersecurity protects computers, networks, accounts, and data from digital attacks. Students and small businesses must understand cyber threats and learn how to protect their information.

Five Common Cyber Threats

1. Phishing

Attackers send fake emails or messages to steal passwords and financial information.

Protection: Check links and sender details before clicking.

2. Malware and Ransomware

Malware is harmful software. Ransomware can lock files and demand money.

Protection: Install updates, use security software, and back up important files.

3. Password Attacks

Attackers steal or guess passwords to access online accounts.

Protection: Use unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication.

4. Online Payment Scams

Criminals use fake payment requests or impersonate trusted people to steal money.

Protection: Verify payment requests before transferring money.

5. Outdated Software

Old software may contain security weaknesses that attackers exploit.

Protection: Update your operating system, apps, and devices.

Cybersecurity Safety Tips

  • Never share passwords or OTPs.
  • Use multi-factor authentication.
  • Avoid suspicious links and attachments.
  • Keep software updated.
  • Back up important data regularly.
  • Verify online payment requests.
